You are an interior architect designing ONE room for the brief below. Produce a complete, buildable design as structured data plus a written specification. Work only from the brief and your own professional judgement.

GEOMETRY CONVENTION (mandatory)
- The room is a rectangle: length_m along x (east), width_m along y (north). Origin (0,0) is the interior south-west corner.
- South wall is y=0, north wall is y=width, west wall is x=0, east wall is x=length. Long walls = north/south. Short walls = west/east.
- Furniture footprints are axis-aligned rectangles: (x_m, y_m) is the SW corner; length_m runs along x; depth_m along y. To rotate an item, swap length and depth.
- Opening offset_m runs along the wall from its LEFT end as seen from inside the room: north wall from the west end; south wall from the east end; west wall from the south end; east wall from the north end.
- Elevation element x_m uses the same left-end convention on the elevation wall; z_m is height above finished floor.
- All dimensions in metres with 2 decimal places. Everything must fit inside the room; footprints must not overlap (rugs and pendant lights may overlap: set allow_overlap true); nothing may sit inside a door swing; the circulation path is a polyline inside the room with at least min_clear_width_m of clear floor either side of its centreline.

DELIVERABLES — write exactly these two files to the output directory given to you:
1. spec.json — must validate against the JSON schema below (every required field, no extra fields). Use `functions` tags honestly (seating items carry `seats`; fireplace/hearth, desk, shelving, bed, sideboard, storage, entry_element, screen, partition, rug, lighting).
2. spec.md — the written spec with these sections in this order: Layout (in words); Dimensions; Palette (hex values); Materials and finishes; Furniture list (item, material, L × D × H, position, built-in or freestanding); Lighting and openings; Decision log (every major decision with a one-line rationale).

QUALITY BAR
- Respect every stated brief constraint and dimension. If something cannot be met, say so explicitly in spec.md rather than silently dropping it.
- Dimensions in spec.md must match spec.json exactly (the SVG drawings are generated from spec.json and checked against the brief).
- Palette: 5–8 swatches, each with a hex value, a plain-language name, a material, a finish and where it is used; include the floor finish.
- Elevation: the principal wall named in the brief, with every element given x, z, width, height and a material label.
- Do not include any designer names, project names or references to real buildings anywhere in the output.

BRIEF
# Iteration Brief I1 — Living room (ITERATION set, not a test brief)

**Room:** Living room, 6.00 m × 4.50 m (interior clear), ceiling 2.40 m.

**Must:**
1. Seat 6 people (seating count, counted on the furniture schedule).
2. Include a fireplace wall (a fireplace/hearth element on one wall).
3. A continuous window band on one long wall (north or south), spanning at least 60% of that wall's length.
4. A defined entry sequence that does not open directly onto the seating area: the entry door's centre must be at least 1.50 m from the nearest seating footprint, and the design must include a named entry element (vestibule, screen, built-in, step or ceiling change) between door and seating.
5. At least one entry door (0.80–1.00 m wide) on a wall of the designer's choice.

**Materials — must:** the floor finish is named with material and finish.
**Materials — must not:** mirrored wall panels; high-gloss lacquer on any wall surface.

**Always:** clear circulation path ≥ 0.90 m from the entry door to the principal seating and to the fireplace; no furniture within any door swing; all furniture inside the room with no overlapping footprints.

**Principal wall for the elevation:** the fireplace wall.
