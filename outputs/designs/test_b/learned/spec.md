# Test Brief B — Study / bedroom — learned arm

## Layout

The room is a 4.20 m × 3.60 m rectangle with its long axis running east–west, and it is organised as three parallel strips along that axis so that it reads as one long, low bar (R01, R02).

**West strip — entry gallery (x 0.00–1.00).** The entry door is a flush oak leaf, 0.90 m wide, set at the south end of the west short wall (offset 0.20 m from the south corner), hinged on the south jamb and opening inward. It has no architrave or surround (R12). The person arrives under a dropped soffit at 2.10 m into a 1.00 m wide gallery; immediately to the north, the built-in closet press (0.60 m deep, running from y 1.20 to the north wall) is the solid mass beside which the vestibule is compressed. The room then opens eastward toward the window band (R12, R02, R06). The gallery floor is red quarry tile; the closet press returns into the desk run at the north-west corner.

**Central strip — study field (x 1.00–3.30).** The full-height ceiling field at 2.40 m. The north wall carries the single built-in run of desk and shelving; the south wall carries the window band with its sill-course planter trough. The desk chair sits in front of the desk knee space.

**East strip — sleeping bay (x 3.30–4.20).** A built-in oak platform bed (0.90 × 2.00) lies along the east brick wall with its head to the north, and a built-in bedside ledge fills the gap between the bed head and the end of the shelving. The bed has no headboard: the horizontally coursed brick wall and its pale stone band at 2.10 m are the backdrop (R14, R08).

**Window band.** The south long wall carries one 3.30 m band from x 0.30 to 3.60 (78.6 % of the wall). It is a ribbon of three glazed bays, each a pair of tall narrow outward-opening casements, alternating with two framed solid stippled panels on a 1.23 m module, with a continuous cast-stone sill course at 0.74 m and its head at 2.10 m directly under the oversailing soffit band (R07, R11, R15, R05). The end piers are unequal (0.30 m west, 0.60 m east) so the wall is not bilaterally symmetrical (R06).

**Head band and ceiling.** A 0.30 m deep dropped soffit at 2.10 m runs round the whole perimeter as a continuous head band at door-head height, oversailing the window heads like an eave; it widens to 1.00 m over the entry gallery. The ceiling field inside it is flat at 2.40 m (R05, R01, R12).

**Circulation.** From the door the path runs east along the window wall, turns north to the desk front and then east to the bed side: [0.10, 0.65] → [1.95, 0.65] → [1.95, 2.55] → [2.85, 2.55], with 0.45 m clear either side of the centreline (0.90 m total) throughout. No furniture sits within the door swing (x 0.00–0.90, y 0.20–1.10).

## Materials and finishes

- **Floor (must):** fumed European oak boards, 180 mm wide, hardwax-oil matt, laid east–west along the long axis. Entry gallery strip (x 0.00–1.00): unglazed red quarry tile 150 × 300 mm, matt penetrating sealer, laid in horizontal running courses.
- **Walls:** east wall, south window-wall piers and the back wall of the desk/shelving run in exposed tawny brick with raked horizontal joints and flush-struck vertical joints, so only horizontal coursing reads (R08). West wall is the closet press and door. All brick tops and edges are capped by a plain pale cast-stone band: the sill course at 0.70–0.74, the plinth at 0.00–0.10, the cap slabs at 2.04–2.10, and a string band at 2.10 on the east wall under the head band (R08). No mirrored panels and no high-gloss lacquer on any wall surface.
- **Ceiling and soffits:** lime plaster with a stippled texture, matt limewash; oak fascia to the head band.
- **Joinery:** quarter-sawn oak, hardwax oil matt, built from stacked rectangular slabs and square posts — an oversailing top slab with a thinner secondary rail beneath, square block feet and plinths, square oak pulls on a grid; no curved profiles, carving or turning (R14).
- **Glazing:** oak casements with straight-line leaded ladder bars and a chevron head; solid infill panels of stippled plaster in oak frames matching the lights (R09, R15).
- **Metal:** copper, mill finish waxed, for the planter liner and the light channels.
- **Upholstery:** oxblood aniline leather over stuffing, waxed.

## Decision log

1. **Three parallel strips along the long axis** — entry/service gallery west under a 2.10 soffit, study field centre, sleeping bay east: keeps the room a long low bar with services gathered in one lower, narrower strip at one end. (R01, R02) — major
2. **Entry door at the south end of the west short wall, flush, no surround, hinged south, opening in** — a plain door at a wall end arriving in a compressed vestibule beside the closet mass, from which the room opens east; door, band and desk share no centreline. (R12, R02, R06) — major
3. **One 3.30 m window band on the south wall, three paired-casement bays alternating with framed stippled panels on a 1.23 module, sill 0.74, head 2.10** — a continuous ribbon of tall narrow lights with a sill course and head at the ceiling band, never a picture window; framed panels stand in for piers so the band is one buildable opening. (R07, R11, R15, R05) — major
4. **Leaded ladder-bar lattice with chevron heads; infill panels framed like the lights** — detailed glass is a straight-line lattice. (R09, R15) — major
5. **Unequal end piers 0.30 / 0.60** — the band stops short of both corners without a symmetrical south wall; the wider east pier backs the bed. (R06, R11) — major
6. **Desk and shelving as one run with a continuous 0.70–0.74 datum slab, full-length shelves at 1.46 and 1.82, pale stone cap at 2.04–2.10, set-back uprights** — slabs run uninterrupted, no vertical element emphasised, oversailing top with a thinner rail beneath, light cap on the built-in. (R01, R14, R08) — major
7. **Storage without freestanding wardrobes: closet press, cupboard bank, pedestal, bed drawers, ledge** — storage belongs in the service strip; square pulls on a grid. (R02, R14) — major
8. **Built-in platform bed on the east wall, head north, no headboard, drawers below; bedside ledge** — low stacked-slab platform against the plain brick wall and its stone band. (R14, R01) — major
9. **Flat 2.40 ceiling field with a 0.30 m perimeter soffit at 2.10 widening to 1.00 m over the gallery** — the ceiling edge is a deep band oversailing the window heads like an eave; flat ceiling; compressed entry. (R05, R01, R12) — major
10. **Brick with horizontal coursing only, every edge capped by a pale stone band; oak of stacked slabs and square posts; no curves, no mirror, no gloss** — brick as horizontal courses with light caps; profiles stepped and rectangular. (R08, R14) — major
11. **Warm palette: tawny brick and oiled oak dominant, pale stone and stippled plaster neutral, copper and oxblood leather accents** — dark bands between light strips, oak, copper and leather, kept warm. (R08, R14) — major
12. **Fumed oak boards east–west; quarry tile in the gallery strip** — boards along the long axis reinforce the horizontal; tile marks the service strip. (R01, R02) — major
13. **Four horizontal datums round the room (0.10, 0.74, 1.50, 2.10); lights 0.42 × 1.36** — continuous sill course and head band keep the room long and low; lights tall and narrow on a regular module. (R01, R07) — major
14. **Lighting as horizontal lines: cove in the band edge, task light under the shelf slab, slot in the gallery soffit, one small square bedside fitting; no pendants** — nothing vertical is emphasised; the band reads as the source. (R01, R05) — major
15. **Copper-lined planter trough on the sill course** — planting built in on the sill, not freestanding pots. (R10) — minor
16. **Oak desk chair with square posts, stepped finials, three-layer crest, block feet, leather seat, tall narrow back** — stacked layers, no carving or turning. (R14) — minor
17. **No fireplace** — the brief asks for none and there is no internal core wall; hearth and chimney rules (R03, R13) are not cited. (no rule) — minor

**Conflicts recorded (rule vs brief)**

- **R04 vs brief** (one window band plus bed, storage and desk wall in 4.20 × 3.60): no end wall is free for a full-width window group; the single band goes on the long south wall. Brief favoured.
- **R07 vs brief** (band must span ≥ 50 % of a wall as measured from the openings): the band is one 3.30 m structural opening (78.6 %) rather than three casement openings between brick piers; the pier rhythm is kept with framed solid panels (R15). Brief favoured on the measured span, rule kept on rhythm.
- **R12 vs brief** (ceiling 2.40 m): the structural ceiling stays at 2.40 m; the compressed vestibule is an applied 0.30 m soffit at 2.10 confined to the gallery and the perimeter band.
- **R11 vs brief** (bedroom with no terrace stated): floor-to-head glazed leaves onto a terrace do not apply; the band is tall outward-opening casements over a 0.74 sill course carrying the planter trough.

**Not applied (not in conflict, simply inapplicable):** R03 and R13 (hearth and chimney — no fireplace in the brief).
