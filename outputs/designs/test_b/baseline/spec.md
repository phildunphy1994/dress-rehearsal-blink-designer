# Window-Wall Study with Corner Daybed

Brief: test_b (Study / bedroom). Arm: baseline.

## Layout

The room is a 4.20 m x 3.60 m rectangle with the long walls to north and south. The whole north wall is given over to joinery: a floor-to-ceiling shelving tower in each corner (0.60 m wide, 0.60 m deep) and a 3.00 m built-in desk spanning between them, directly under a 3.00 m window band. The desk, the two towers, the drawer band and the blind pelmet are all built in and form one composition on the principal wall.

The entry door is on the east wall near the south-east corner (leaf at y 0.15-1.05), opening into the room and hinged on the south jamb so the leaf folds back toward the south wall. North of the door, a floor-to-ceiling built-in wardrobe runs along the east wall (y 1.15-3.00) and meets the east tower at y 3.00, so the east and north walls read as an L of green joinery. The wardrobe starts 0.10 m north of the door swing; nothing sits inside the swing quadrant (x 3.30-4.20, y 0.15-1.05).

A 2.00 m x 1.20 m daybed with drawers sits in the south-west corner, head to the west wall and long side against the south wall, with bolsters against the wall so it reads as a sofa by day. A small bedside table stands against the west wall just north of the daybed head, under a swing-arm reading light. The centre of the room, about 1.80 m x 3.00 m between daybed and desk, is left open with a wool rug; the desk chair sits at the centre of the window band.

Circulation: from the door centre the path runs west along the south side of the room (1.15 m clear between south wall and wardrobe), turns north past the wardrobe (1.60 m clear between daybed foot and wardrobe), runs west along the daybed's open north side (1.25 m clear between daybed and chair) and ends 0.50 m in front of the desk. Every segment keeps at least 0.45 m clear either side of the centreline, so the 0.90 m requirement from door to bed and door to desk is met.

## Materials and finishes

- Floor: engineered oak plank, 180 mm boards, matt hardwax-oil finish, laid along the long (x) axis.
- Walls and ceiling: plaster with mineral paint, dead-matt chalk white. No mirrored wall panels and no high-gloss lacquer on any wall surface.
- Joinery (towers, desk drawer band, wardrobe, pelmet): MDF carcasses and doors, bottle-green matt eggshell; oak internal fittings.
- Desk worktop and tower shelves: 40 mm solid oak, matt hardwax oil. Worktop carried on the towers and a concealed steel wall rail.
- Pinboard strip: linen-faced cork between worktop and sill.
- Window: timber-framed double glazing, three lights, charcoal hardware; roller blind in the pelmet.
- Daybed: oak-veneered plywood platform with two drawers, matt hardwax oil; flax linen mattress cover and bolsters.
- Rug: undyed wool flatweave.
- Chair: charcoal matte powder-coated steel frame with oak-veneer moulded plywood seat.

## Decision log

1. Desk and both shelving towers on the north (window) wall. Daylight falls on the worktop, the same-wall requirement is met, and the towers frame the window band as one piece of joinery.
2. Window band 3.00 m wide between the towers (71% of the wall), sill 0.90 m above the 0.74 m worktop. Exceeds the 50% minimum while leaving solid wall for the towers; the 0.16 m gap takes a pinboard.
3. Entry door on the east wall near the south-east corner, hinged on the south jamb. The leaf folds back toward the south wall, the view on entry is to the window wall, and the swing sits in a corner no furniture needs.
4. Storage as a floor-to-ceiling built-in wardrobe on the east wall, flush with the east tower. Meets the no-freestanding-wardrobe rule, completes the L of joinery, and keeps 0.10 m clear of the door swing.
5. Daybed in the south-west corner, head to the west wall, back to the south wall. Sleeps one, reads as a sofa by day, and its drawers add storage while leaving the room centre open.
6. Circulation as a 0.90 m clear path from the door along the south side, past the wardrobe, along the daybed's open side to the desk. Both bed and desk are reached directly; the open floor between them stays 1.60-1.80 m wide.
7. Palette of pale oak floor, chalk-white matt walls and bottle-green matt joinery with oak worktop. Dark matt joinery gives the low room depth without reflection; no mirrors, no gloss on walls.
8. Layered lighting: pelmet task light, tower strips, wall reading light, two surface-mounted discs. The 2.40 m ceiling rules out pendants in the walk zone.
9. Freestanding chair and rug rather than built-in seating at the desk. The chair can move to the daybed; the rug anchors the floor without blocking drawers.

All brief constraints are met; nothing was dropped.
