# Dining room, Iteration Brief I2 — baseline arm

Banquette dining room: a bench wall with flanking built-in sideboards.

## Layout

The room is a 4.80 m × 3.60 m rectangle with a 2.40 m ceiling; the long walls are north and south, the short walls west and east. The whole north long wall is one continuous piece of built-in joinery: a 2.40 m upholstered banquette in the centre, flanked by two 1.15 m built-in sideboards at 0.90 m counter height, so the bench sits exactly centred on the wall and the sideboard side panels close its ends. The 2.40 m × 0.84 m dining table stands 10 mm off the banquette seat front, centred on the room length, with four chairs along its south side. Four people sit on the banquette and four on the chairs: eight dining seats.

The kitchen door is on the west short wall and the hall door on the east short wall, both centred at y = 0.90 near the south end of their walls. Between them runs a straight 1.80 m wide circulation corridor along the south wall (centreline y = 0.90, with 0.90 m of clear floor on each side: the south wall on one side, the chair backs at y = 1.81 on the other). Both doors swing into the room and their arcs fall entirely inside that corridor. The 2.40 m window band on the south wall sits directly opposite the table and banquette, so daylight falls across the table and onto the bench wall.

Both table ends are left open. The west end, next to the kitchen door and the west sideboard, is the service landing where dishes arrive and where people slide into the banquette; the east end gives access to the east sideboard (glassware and linen) and the other bench entry.

## Materials and finishes

- Floor: engineered oak plank, 180 mm wide boards, matt hardwax-oil finish, laid along the room length (x). This is the named floor finish.
- Walls: clay-based mineral emulsion, matt, in Chalk. No mirrored wall panels anywhere in the room and no high-gloss lacquer on any wall surface, as the brief requires.
- Ceiling: flat plaster, matt acrylic emulsion in Soft white.
- Built-in joinery (banquette box, both sideboards): birch plywood carcasses with MDF doors and drawer fronts, painted matt eggshell in Moss grey (low sheen, applied to furniture only, not to wall surfaces); 40 mm solid fumed oak counter tops with hardwax oil; a 200 mm deep fumed oak shelf above the bench at 1.55 m.
- Upholstery: wool bouclé in Sand over foam on the banquette seat and back cushions and on the chair seat pads; the banquette seat lifts for storage.
- Table: solid fumed oak top, hardwax oil, on a brushed blackened-steel trestle base.
- Chairs: solid fumed oak frames with upholstered seat pads.
- Metalwork: brushed and waxed blackened steel for the pendant, wall lights, table base, cabinet handles and door and window ironmongery.
- Doors: flush painted leaves to match the walls; window: timber frame painted Chalk.

## Decision log

1. Table along the long axis, centred at x 2.40, 2.40 × 0.84 m (major). The only orientation that seats eight in a 3.60 m wide room while leaving a continuous route between the two short-wall doors.
2. Built-in banquette on the north wall seating four, 0.49 m deep (major). Satisfies the built-in seating requirement and saves about 0.40 m of depth against a second row of chairs, which is what makes the 1.80 m corridor possible.
3. Two built-in sideboards flanking the banquette at 0.90 m counter height, filling the north wall (major). One continuous joinery wall gives a serving counter by the kitchen door, glassware and linen storage, closes the bench ends, and forms the principal elevation.
4. Kitchen door on the west short wall and hall door on the east short wall, both centred at y 0.90 (major). Doors land at the corridor ends so the route is straight and both inward swing arcs lie within the corridor, clear of every piece of furniture.
5. Circulation corridor 1.80 m wide along the south wall, centreline y 0.90, nearest furniture face at y 1.81 (major). Gives 0.90 m of clear floor on each side of the centreline, meeting the 0.90 m brief minimum under either reading of the clearance rule.
6. Four chairs on the south side only, no end chairs (major). Keeps both table ends open for service and for entry to the banquette; end chairs would trap the corner bench seats.
7. South window band 2.40 m wide matched to the table, sill 0.95 m, head 2.10 m (major). Provides the second opening and daylight opposite the bench wall; head aligned with the door heads for one datum under the low ceiling.
8. Light envelope with dark joinery kept below 1.05 m (major). A 2.40 m ceiling reads taller when the upper two thirds of the room are light; the moss-grey band anchors the bench wall.
9. One linear pendant at 1.55 m underside plus shelf strip, two wall lights and two corridor downlights (minor). Even light along the whole table without a cluster of pendants under a low ceiling; separate circuits give task and ambient layers.
10. No mirrored panels; matt clay paint on walls, matt eggshell on joinery only (minor). Complies with both must-not items in the brief.
11. Floor named as engineered oak plank, 180 mm boards, matt hardwax oil, laid along x (minor). Satisfies the floor-finish requirement; long boards stretch the plan and matt oil suits daily chair movement.

Constraints not met: none. All brief constraints (eight dining seats, built-in seating and sideboards, 0.90 m kitchen door on a short wall, additional window and hall door, named floor finish, no mirrors or gloss on walls, 0.90 m circulation, no furniture in door swings, no overlapping footprints) are satisfied.
