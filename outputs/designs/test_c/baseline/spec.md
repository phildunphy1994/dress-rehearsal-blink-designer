# Dining-living room with end-wall hearth and dropped dining canopy

Brief: test_c (Dining / living combined). Arm: baseline.

## Layout (in words)

The room is a 7.20 m by 4.50 m rectangle with a 2.40 m ceiling. You enter at the south end of the west wall and look straight down the room along a gallery on the south wall to the hearth, which is centred on the east end wall. The gallery is the circulation path: a straight strip along the south wall, kept completely free of furniture, that runs from the entry door to a slot window beside the chimney breast.

North of the gallery the room divides into two zones with no partition of any kind.

The **dining zone** occupies the west end (x 0.00-3.75). The dining table is turned across the room, long axis north-south, centred on x 1.85. Three chairs sit on each long side, one chair at the south head facing up the table, and a built-in upholstered banquette along the north wall takes the north head seat (it also spans the chair rows and stores linen under a lift-up seat). The zone is marked overhead by a plasterboard canopy dropped 0.15 m to 2.25 m, 2.50 m by 3.20 m, returning to the north wall; its soffit is painted a dark moss slate and a concealed LED cove runs in the step. The gallery stays at full height.

The **living zone** occupies the east end (x 3.75-7.20). A three-seat sofa faces east towards the fire; its back, at x 3.75, is the line between the zones and gives the diners on the east side of the table 0.88 m to draw their chairs. In front of the sofa a limestone coffee table sits on a wool rug; an armchair and side table sit on the north side turned towards the fire; a floor lamp stands at the sofa's north arm. The east wall is the hearth wall: a full-height chimney breast with a limestone surround and a blackened steel insert, a projecting limestone hearth slab, full-height oak joinery (log niche below, open shelves above) on the north flank, and a slot window on the south flank where the gallery ends.

The level/ceiling requirement is met by the **ceiling change**: the dining canopy at 2.25 m against the main 2.40 m ceiling, located on plan at x 0.60-3.10, y 1.30-4.50. There is no level change (see decision log).

## Materials and finishes

- **Floor:** engineered oak plank, 190 mm boards laid along x, matt hardwax-oil finish (#D2B48C). Continuous through both zones and the gallery; no threshold at the zone line.
- **Walls:** skimmed plaster with matt mineral (silicate) paint, Chalk (#EDE6D6). Skirting 100 mm painted softwood to match. No mirrored panels; no gloss or high-gloss lacquer on any wall surface.
- **Main ceiling:** skimmed and painted Chalk, matt, at 2.40 m.
- **Dining canopy:** plasterboard bulkhead on metal framing, 0.15 m drop to 2.25 m, soffit and fascia in matt Moss slate emulsion (#3F4A44); concealed dimmable LED cove in the step on the west, south and east edges.
- **Chimney breast:** blockwork, lime plaster painted Chalk; honed limestone surround (#B9AE9A); blackened steel closed-combustion insert and 50 mm steel mantel shelf (#2A2A28), waxed.
- **Hearth slab:** honed limestone, 50 mm thick, 20 mm movement joint at the breast face.
- **Flank joinery:** smoked oak veneer on MDF carcass, matt oiled (#8B6F4E); log niche lined in blackened steel; three open oak shelves.
- **Dining furniture:** solid smoked oak table top on blackened steel trestles; solid oak chairs with woven paper-cord seats; banquette carcass in oak veneer with olive wool felt cushions (#6E7257) and lift-up seat storage.
- **Living furniture:** sofa and armchair in terracotta wool boucle (#A9583A); coffee table limestone top on blackened steel frame; side table solid smoked oak; rug olive wool flatweave; floor lamp blackened steel with linen shade.
- **Windows:** painted timber frames (Chalk), clear double glazing.

## Decision log

1. **Dining table turned across the room, banquette as north head seat (major).** A 2.20 m table along x with two chair pull-outs needs about 5.00 m and leaves no living zone; across the room it needs 3.75 m, the banquette removes one pull-out, and eight seats fit (3+3 on the sides, one chair, one banquette place).
2. **Ceiling change, not level change (major).** With only 2.40 m there is no headroom to give up under a step, and a platform would cut the end-to-end gallery; a 0.15 m canopy at 2.25 m over x 0.60-3.10 / y 1.30-4.50 marks the dining zone, carries the pendant and cove, and leaves gallery and living at full height.
3. **Hearth on the east end wall, sofa facing it, sofa back as the zone edge (major).** One axis from the entry door to the fire; the two zones share the room's length with no partition, and the long south wall stays free for windows.
4. **Gallery along the south wall, nothing south of y 1.15 (major).** A straight 1.15-1.31 m clear route from the west wall to the east wall, over the 0.90 m minimum, walking past windows rather than through seating.
5. **Entry door at the south end of the west wall, hinged south (major).** You enter at the head of the gallery; the swing quadrant lies in the gallery where nothing stands and the leaf folds back towards the south wall.
6. **Asymmetric hearth wall: joinery north, slot window south (major).** Joinery on both flanks would block the east end of the gallery; a window there ends the route on daylight and side-lights the hearth.
7. **All wall and ceiling finishes matt; no mirror, no gloss (minor).** Meets the materials exclusions and avoids glare under a low ceiling.
8. **Rug 1.80 x 2.20 stopping 0.08 m short of the hearth (minor).** Keeps textile off the fire while the sofa front legs and coffee table sit on it; the rug stays out of the gallery.
9. **20 mm gaps between abutting footprints (minor).** Chairs to table, banquette to table, hearth to breast, joinery to breast stay distinct for the overlap check and match the real shadow gaps and movement joints.

Constraints checked: two named zones with no partition; ceiling change located on plan; 8 dining seats on the schedule; fireplace and hearth present; 0.90 m path end wall to end wall (1.15 m provided); entry door 0.90 m plus three windows; floor finish named with material and finish; no mirrored panels or gloss lacquer; no furniture in the door swing; all footprints inside the room and non-overlapping (rug and pendant flagged allow_overlap). Nothing in the brief was dropped. The one point to note in use is that the south head chair, when drawn out 0.40 m, narrows the gallery to 0.91 m, which still meets the minimum.
