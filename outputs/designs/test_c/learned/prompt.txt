You are an interior architect designing ONE room for the brief below. Produce a complete, buildable design as structured data plus a written specification. Work only from the brief and your own professional judgement.

GEOMETRY CONVENTION (mandatory)
- The room is a rectangle: length_m along x (east), width_m along y (north). Origin (0,0) is the interior south-west corner.
- South wall is y=0, north wall is y=width, west wall is x=0, east wall is x=length. Long walls = north/south. Short walls = west/east.
- Furniture footprints are axis-aligned rectangles: (x_m, y_m) is the SW corner; length_m runs along x; depth_m along y. To rotate an item, swap length and depth.
- Opening offset_m runs along the wall from its LEFT end as seen from inside the room: north wall from the west end; south wall from the east end; west wall from the south end; east wall from the north end.
- Elevation element x_m uses the same left-end convention on the elevation wall; z_m is height above finished floor.
- All dimensions in metres with 2 decimal places. Everything must fit inside the room; footprints must not overlap (rugs and pendant lights may overlap: set allow_overlap true); nothing may sit inside a door swing; the circulation path is a polyline inside the room whose clear width (wall to furniture, or furniture to furniture, measured across the path) is at least min_clear_width_m in total, i.e. 0.45 m either side of the centreline for a 0.90 m path.

## Do-not list
- Does not express a principal entrance: no exterior door on the main floor (00003, 00017), no door on the main block in the long elevations (00019, 00020), and no steps, path, porch roof or canopy at any door drawn (00005, 00006). At room scale: no door surround, no centred door opposite the hearth; the door is flush and arrives in a compressed vestibule (02, 16).
- Does not compose a bilaterally symmetrical elevation: every whole-building elevation is asymmetrical and stepped (00006, 00019, 00020, 00021); symmetry is used only within a single element — the west fireplace face (00007), one window light (00008) and chair fronts (00012, 00013).
- Does not use carving, turning, applied ornament or a decorative mantel: fireplaces are plain brick openings with no overmantel or surround (00007), the chair has no carving or turning beyond its layered cap and moulded foot (00013), and frames, posts and spindles are drawn in plain outline (00012).
- Does not draw vertical joints in brickwork: every brick surface is rendered as continuous horizontal coursing only (00005, 00006, 00019, 00020, 00021).
- Does not use gabled or steep roofs: every roof drawn is a very low-pitched hipped roof with a deep overhang and a thin fascia (00005, 00006, 00019, 00020, 00021).
- Does not place the hearth on an outside wall: the fireplace is always in the central masonry core on the long axis between the two main rooms (00003, 00005, 00017; 'central hearth' in the printed description on 00001). At room scale: where a brief demands a 'fireplace wall', choose the wall that reads as internal (shared with the adjoining room or stair) and build the hearth as a masonry mass with passage past it, not as a surround on the exterior wall (02, 16).
