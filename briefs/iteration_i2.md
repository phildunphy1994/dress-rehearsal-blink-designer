# Iteration Brief I2 — Dining room (ITERATION set, not a test brief)

**Room:** Dining room, 4.80 m × 3.60 m (interior clear), ceiling 2.40 m.

**Must:**
1. Seat 8 people for dining (dining seats counted on the furniture schedule).
2. Include built-in seating or a built-in sideboard (at least one built-in item with function seating or sideboard).
3. A direct connection to a kitchen doorway on one short wall (west or east): a door 0.80–1.00 m wide on a short wall, labelled as the kitchen door.
4. At least one further opening of the designer's choice (window or doorway) so the room is not a sealed box.

**Materials — must:** the floor finish is named with material and finish.
**Materials — must not:** mirrored wall panels; high-gloss lacquer on any wall surface.

**Always:** clear circulation path ≥ 0.90 m from the kitchen door around the table to the room's other door/opening; no furniture within any door swing; all furniture inside the room with no overlapping footprints.

**Principal wall for the elevation:** the built-in wall (seating or sideboard).
